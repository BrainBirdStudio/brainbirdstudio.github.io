Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Applies to the application “Brain Bird Studio”

1. Who we are

Brain Bird Studio is the publishing tool of Brain Bird, a creator of English-language short documentaries about science, history and unexplained mysteries. The application is used only to publish videos that Brain Bird produces itself to social media accounts that Brain Bird owns or is authorized to manage, including TikTok accounts connected through TikTok’s official APIs.

2. What the application does

When an authorized account holder connects a TikTok account, Brain Bird Studio uses TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0) and the TikTok Content Posting API to:

  • identify the connected TikTok account (basic profile information), and
  • upload and publish Brain Bird’s own finished videos to that account, and check the status of those posts.

The application does not read other users’ content, does not send messages, does not follow, like or comment, and does not run advertising.

3. Data we process

  • TikTok account information provided by TikTok after you authorize the application (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar. If additional scopes are ever requested, they will be listed here before use.
  • Authorization data: the OAuth access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, their expiry times and the granted scopes.
  • Publishing data: the videos, captions and post settings (for example privacy level, comment/duet/stitch settings and commercial-content disclosure) that the account holder selects, the publish ID and the post status returned by TikTok.
  • Creator information returned by TikTok before each post (for example available privacy options and maximum video length), used only to show correct posting options.

We do not collect TikTok passwords. We do not process data of TikTok users other than the account holder who connected the account.

4. Purposes and legal basis

  • Connecting the account and publishing the account holder’s own videos at their request – Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the service requested) and, where applicable, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent given in the TikTok authorization screen).
  • Keeping the connection secure, detecting errors and expired authorizations – Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure and working publishing process).

TikTok data is used solely to provide these functions. We do not sell or rent personal data, do not build profiles of individuals and do not use the data for advertising.

5. Storage and security

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only locally on the operator’s protected workstation, outside any public or shared repository, and are never written to logs, reports or published files.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok are short-lived (currently about 2 hours); refresh tokens currently remain valid for up to about 180 days unless revoked. We use the actual expiry values returned by TikTok.
  • Data is transmitted to TikTok only over encrypted HTTPS connections using TikTok’s official API endpoints.

6. Retention

  • Tokens are kept only while the connection is active and are deleted when the authorization is revoked, when they expire without renewal, or when the connection is no longer needed.
  • Records of published posts (publish ID, post status, date and the published video itself) are kept as part of our publishing records for as long as the video is published and for documentation of our own content.
  • When the use of TikTok’s developer services ends, all information obtained from TikTok will be deleted.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering enquiries) and your consent given in the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The provider’s own privacy policy applies to that page. Provider information: https://formcarry.com/legal/data-processing-agreement

We delete form submissions from the form service as soon as your request has been answered, at the latest after three months, unless statutory retention obligations apply. The form uses no tracking and sets no cookies on this website; a hidden field is used only to filter automated spam.

7. Recipients

  • TikTok receives the videos and post settings we publish and processes your data under its own privacy policy as an independent controller.
  • Website hosting: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which may process technical access data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the pages. This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Where data is processed outside the EU/EEA (for example by TikTok or the hosting provider), this happens on the basis of the safeguards those providers offer, such as EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Revoking authorization and deleting data

  • You can revoke Brain Bird Studio’s access at any time in your TikTok app settings, in the section that manages authorized apps and permissions.
  • When the connection is removed, we also revoke the token through TikTok’s official revoke endpoint and delete the stored tokens.
  • You can request deletion of any data we hold about you by email to timsussnapp@gmail.com.
